# Paired throughput probe -- dense/MoE split, 15 Aug 2026

Box `sb-517fce9fbd0e6932` (RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell), BiBo `cb41584`, `ablate/tools/tps_probe.sh`.
150 steps per run, val off, no W&B. **A B C interleaved twice** so machine drift shows up as
pass1 != pass2 rather than as a config effect.

| arm | dense layers | pass 1 | pass 2 | median | vs A |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | `0,9`  | 179.6k | 179.6k | **179.6k** | -- |
| B | `0`    | 175.1k | 175.1k | **175.1k** | -2.5% |
| C | none   | 170.9k | 170.8k | **170.9k** | -4.9% |

Pass 1 and pass 2 agree to 0.1k on every arm. `SW Power Capping` counter read 1218723 us before
AND after the probe -- unchanged, so no clock throttling occurred during it and the numbers stand.

**Each dense->MoE swap costs a consistent ~2.5%.** The 11x asymmetry seen in the actual training
runs (35 ms vs 390 ms per swap) was entirely box drift; see memory `box-tps-drift`. Do NOT quote
tps from the ffn-A/B/C training runs -- those were measured hours apart on a box that moved 20%.

At this exchange rate the 4.9% is cheap: spending it on A instead buys ~98 extra steps, worth
~0.010 val at A's end-of-run slope, against the 0.066 val that all-MoE delivers.

Recorded here because the mirroring loop that was supposed to save the raw logs wrote an empty
file, and the box has since been terminated. These medians are the surviving record.
